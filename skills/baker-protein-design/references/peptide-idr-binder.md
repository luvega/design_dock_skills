# Peptide and IDR binder

1. Record whether the target is a fixed peptide conformation, a flexible helix, an extended IDR, or a sequence-only IDP.
2. Define residues that must remain accessible or contacted and whether target conformational selection is acceptable.
3. Use the matching flexible-target or joint target-binder generation recipe; do not force an IDR into a single unvalidated structure.
4. Design binder sequences, predict ensembles, and assess interface confidence and binder monomer quality separately.
5. Counter-screen a scrambled peptide, homologous IDRs, relevant point mutants, and the full cross-specificity matrix.
6. Experimentally test binding in a context that preserves target disorder and relevant post-translational states.

Sequence-only diffusion is not evidence that the sampled target conformation is the cellular ensemble. Report it as a model hypothesis.
