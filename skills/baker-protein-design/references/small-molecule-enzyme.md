# Small-molecule and enzyme design

1. Freeze the ligand identity, stereochemistry, protonation, tautomer, covalent state, metal coordination, and atom naming.
2. For binding, define pocket geometry and key polar/nonpolar contacts. For catalysis, define reaction-coordinate ensembles and catalytic residue geometry before generating a scaffold.
3. Generate ligand-aware backbones with the declared all-atom or active-site-conditioned model.
4. Design sequences with LigandMPNN or another context-aware sequence tool.
5. Validate whole-structure confidence separately from local ligand geometry; PLACER-like scores remain computational.
6. Check apo-state stability, competing poses, ligand strain, solvent exposure, and negative ligands.
7. For sensors, proceed to circular permutation, insertion or fusion libraries, reporter coupling, and experimental dynamic-range selection.

A one-round binder calculation is not an allosteric switch. Catalytic geometry is not a measured rate.
