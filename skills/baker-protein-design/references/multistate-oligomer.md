# Multistate and oligomer design

Record every positive and negative state, symmetry group, stoichiometry, chain identity, and allowed conformational change. Score the same sequence across all states and preserve the state-specific objective terms. Validate unintended oligomers, aggregation, and off-pathway structures. A symmetry classifier such as Seq2Symm is a routing aid, not experimental proof of assembly.

For `run`, require state-specific command templates and output directories so files from different states cannot be merged silently.
