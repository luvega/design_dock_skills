# Allosteric switch

Treat switch engineering as a multi-stage experimental program:

1. Obtain or design a receptor with verified ligand binding and an apo/holo structural hypothesis.
2. Select reporter, enzyme, split-protein, or signaling outputs and define the required dynamic range.
3. Design insertion, circular-permutation, fusion, linker, and orientation libraries.
4. Screen both OFF and ON states with negative ligands and receptor mutants.
5. Map coupling by structure, mutagenesis, kinetics, HDX-MS, NMR, or another suitable assay.
6. Only call a construct a switch after experimental state-dependent output is measured.

The 2026 artificial-switch paper is a casebook example of receptor insertion and experimental library selection, not evidence that a single generative-model pass yields a switch.
